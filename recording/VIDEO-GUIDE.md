## Step-by-step

### 1. Add Company & Theme  `0:06`

_Company add karein aur har company ka alag theme rang set karein._

- `0:10` This is the Company List — the first screen when AY Toys opens.  
  _App khulte hi Company List aati hai._
- `0:14` Click "Add Company". Type the company name and contact details.  
  _"Add Company" par click karein, naam aur details likhein._
- `0:24` Pick a theme colour for this company — here we choose Ruby Red for XYZ Toys.  
  _Is company ka theme rang chunein — XYZ Toys ke liye laal (red)._
- `0:30` The preview shows how the app will look. You can also pick any custom colour or Dark mode.  
  _Preview mein dekh lein. Custom color aur Dark mode bhi mojood hai._
- `0:38` For this demo we tick "Load sample data" so we have customers, items, invoices and bills to work with.  
  _Demo ke liye sample data load kar rahe hain._
- `0:46` The whole interface is now red — the XYZ Toys theme.  
  _Ab poora app laal rang mein hai — XYZ Toys ka theme._

### 2. Home Page & Insights  `0:49`

_Home page aur Insights dashboard._

- `0:53` The Home Page shows the QuickBooks workflow: Vendors, Customers, Company and Banking. Click any icon to open that task.  
  _Home page par Vendors, Customers, Company aur Banking ka flow hai. Kisi bhi icon par click karein._
- `1:02` On the right: bank balances and Reminders — overdue invoices, bills due, low stock, purchase orders to receive.  
  _Right side par bank balance aur reminders: overdue invoices, bills, low stock._
- `1:12` Insights: income, net income, what customers owe you and what you owe vendors — plus a 12-month income vs expenses chart.  
  _Insights mein income, profit, receivable aur payable ek nazar mein._
- `1:21` Top customers and best-selling items. Click any bar or tile to open the report behind it.  
  _Top customers aur best-selling items. Kisi bhi cheez par click karke report kholen._

### 3. Chart of Accounts  `1:29`

_Chart of Accounts — Ctrl+A dabayein._

- `1:35` Accounts are grouped by QuickBooks type: Bank, Receivable, Inventory, Payable, Equity, Income, Cost of Goods Sold and Expenses.  
  _Har account apni type ke sath: Bank, Receivable, Inventory, Payable, Income, Expense._
- `1:45` Use the arrow keys to move. Press Ctrl+N (or Insert) to add a new account.  
  _Arrow keys se move karein. Naya account: Ctrl+N ya Insert._
- `1:55` We add an Expense account "Warehouse Security". You can also make it a sub-account of another account.  
  _Expense account "Warehouse Security" bana rahe hain._
- `2:03` Double-click a bank account to open its register, or press Ctrl+Q for a QuickReport of the selected account.  
  _Bank account par double-click = register. Ctrl+Q = QuickReport._
- `2:11` The register shows every payment and deposit with a running balance — just like QuickBooks.  
  _Register mein har payment aur deposit running balance ke sath._

### 4. Add Inventory  `2:19`

_Item List aur naya stock item opening quantity ke sath._

- `2:25` The Item List shows quantity on hand, on sales order, on purchase order, average cost, price and stock value.  
  _Item List mein on-hand qty, average cost, price aur stock value._
- `2:32` Items below their reorder point are shown in red with a warning.  
  _Reorder point se kam stock wale items laal mein dikhte hain._
- `2:39` New Item: choose the type "Inventory Part", then the name, SKU, category and unit.  
  _New Item: type "Inventory Part", naam, SKU, category aur unit._
- `2:55` Purchase cost and sales price — AY Toys shows the markup and margin for you.  
  _Purchase cost aur sale price — markup aur margin khud nikal aata hai._
- `3:07` Set a reorder point and the opening quantity you already have in the warehouse.  
  _Reorder point aur warehouse mein mojood opening quantity likhein._
- `3:18` The new item is in the list with 200 pcs on hand at Rs 250 average cost.  
  _Naya item 200 pcs ke sath list mein aa gaya._

### 5. Customer Center  `3:25`

_Customer add karein — terms, price level aur credit limit._

- `3:31` Customer Center: the list with balances on the left, details and every transaction on the right.  
  _Left par customers aur balance, right par details aur transactions._
- `3:39` Statuses show what is Paid, Open or Overdue. Double-click a transaction to open it.  
  _Paid, Open, Overdue status. Double-click se transaction khulti hai._
- `3:56` Enter the customer details. Then open "Payment Settings".  
  _Customer ki details likhein, phir "Payment Settings" kholen._
- `4:05` Price level "Wholesale (-10%)" means this customer automatically gets 10% off the base price on every invoice.  
  _Price level "Wholesale -10%": har invoice par khud 10% kam rate lagega._

### 6. Create Invoice  `4:17`

_Invoice banayein — Ctrl+I, sirf keyboard se._

- `4:23` Type part of the customer name and press Enter. Bill-to, terms, due date and rep fill in automatically.  
  _Customer ka naam likh kar Enter. Address, terms aur due date khud aa jate hain._
- `4:32` In the Item column type a few letters — the list searches by name or SKU and shows stock on hand.  
  _Item column mein naam ya SKU likhein — stock bhi saath dikhta hai._
- `4:43` Rate is 405 — the base price 450 minus the Wholesale 10%. The side panel shows stock, last price and margin.  
  _Rate 405 hai — 450 mein se 10% wholesale discount. Side panel mein stock aur margin._
- `4:55` Amount fields work like a calculator — type 2*12 and press Tab.  
  _Amount fields calculator ki tarah — 2*12 likh kar Tab._
- `5:00` Totals update instantly. Now press Alt+S to Save & New (or Alt+A to Save & Close).  
  _Total foran update. Alt+S = Save & New, Alt+A = Save & Close._
- `5:08` Saved! The form is ready for the next invoice. Alt+P goes back to the previous one.  
  _Save ho gayi! Alt+P se pichli invoice wapas._
- `5:15` Ctrl+Y shows the transaction journal — the debits and credits behind this invoice, including Cost of Goods Sold.  
  _Ctrl+Y: is invoice ka journal — debit aur credit, COGS ke sath._
- `5:26` Use Print for a printable invoice, Memorize (Ctrl+M) for repeat orders, and History (Ctrl+H) for linked payments.  
  _Print, Memorize (Ctrl+M) aur History (Ctrl+H) bhi yahin hain._

### 7. Receive Payments  `5:35`

_Customer se aayi payment record karein._

- `5:41` Clicking "Receive Pmt" opens Receive Payments with this customer and invoice already selected.  
  _"Receive Pmt" se payment window customer aur invoice ke sath khulti hai._
- `5:46` Let’s say Joy Toys paid Rs 30,000 now. Type the amount — it is auto-applied to the oldest invoice.  
  _Joy Toys ne 30,000 diye. Amount likhein — khud oldest invoice par lag jata hai._
- `6:05` The invoice now shows the payment applied and the remaining balance due.  
  _Invoice par payment aur baqi balance nazar aata hai._

### 8. Purchase Order → Receive → Bill  `6:09`

_Vendor se maal order karein, receive karein aur bill banayein._

- `6:15` Teddy Bears are low on stock. We create a Purchase Order to Karachi Plastic Works.  
  _Teddy Bear ka stock kam hai — Purchase Order bana rahe hain._
- `6:30` Ordered quantities show as "On P.O." in the Item List until they arrive.  
  _Order ki hui qty Item List mein "On P.O." dikhti hai._
- `6:34` When the goods arrive, click "Receive & Bill" — the bill is created from the PO.  
  _Maal aane par "Receive & Bill" — PO se bill khud ban jata hai._
- `6:41` The Items tab is filled from the PO. Saving adds 100 bears to stock and updates the average cost.  
  _Items tab PO se bhar gaya. Save par stock aur average cost update._

### 9. Add Bill & Pay Bills  `6:53`

_Vendor ka expense bill aur bills ki payment._

- `6:59` Enter Bills: choose the vendor. Terms and due date come from the vendor.  
  _Enter Bills: vendor chunein — terms aur due date khud aate hain._
- `7:08` On the Expenses tab choose the expense account and amount — here the electricity bill under Utilities.  
  _Expenses tab mein account aur amount — bijli ka bill "Utilities"._
- `7:26` Pay Bills lists every unpaid bill with its due date. Overdue ones are red.  
  _Pay Bills mein saare unpaid bills — overdue laal._
- `7:34` Tick the bills to pay, choose the bank account, and click "Pay Selected Bills".  
  _Bills tick karein, bank account chunein, "Pay Selected Bills"._

### 10. Sales Receipt, Deposit & Checks  `7:42`

_Cash sale, bank deposit, checks aur stock adjustment._

- `7:48` A Sales Receipt is a cash sale — paid on the spot. The money waits in Undeposited Funds.  
  _Sales Receipt = cash sale. Paisa "Undeposited Funds" mein jata hai._
- `8:05` Make Deposits: select the payments you are taking to the bank and save.  
  _Make Deposits: jo payments bank le ja rahe hain unhe select karein._
- `8:17` Write Checks (Alt+K — the browser reserves Ctrl+W): pay expenses straight from the bank, like warehouse rent.  
  _Write Checks (Alt+K): seedha bank se kharcha — jaise kiraya._
- `8:37` Adjust Quantity on Hand: record damaged or missing stock. The value goes to Inventory Shrinkage.  
  _Toota hua ya kam stock adjust karein — "Inventory Shrinkage" mein jata hai._

### 11. Reports  `8:52`

_Customer/Vendor balance, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet aur bohat se reports._

- `8:58` Customer Balance Summary: how much every customer owes you right now.  
  _Customer Balance Summary: har customer ka baqaya._
- `9:02` Double-click any line to QuickZoom into the details.  
  _Kisi bhi line par double-click — detail khul jati hai._
- `9:07` Customer Balance Detail: every invoice and payment with a running balance.  
  _Customer Balance Detail: har invoice aur payment running balance ke sath._
- `9:15` Vendor Balance Summary: how much you owe each vendor.  
  _Vendor Balance Summary: aap ne kis vendor ko kitna dena hai._
- `9:21` Profit & Loss (Income Statement): Income, Cost of Goods Sold, Gross Profit, Expenses and Net Income.  
  _Profit & Loss: income, COGS, gross profit, expenses aur net income._
- `9:28` Change the date range from the Dates list: This Month, Last Quarter, This Fiscal Year and more.  
  _Dates list se date range badlein — This Month, Last Quarter waghera._
- `9:36` Double-click an amount — here Sales — to see every transaction behind it.  
  _Kisi amount par double-click — us ke peeche ki transactions._
- `9:47` Balance Sheet: Assets = Liabilities + Equity. Net Income for the year is included in Equity.  
  _Balance Sheet: Assets = Liabilities + Equity._
- `9:56` Every report can be printed, exported to Excel and memorized with its settings.  
  _Har report Print, Excel export aur Memorize ho sakti hai._
- `10:07` Report Center: 50+ reports grouped like QuickBooks — aging, sales by item, inventory valuation, trial balance…  
  _Report Center: 50 se zyada reports — aging, sales by item, inventory waghera._
- `10:19` Stock Status shows what to reorder and a suggested quantity for each item.  
  _Stock Status: kya reorder karna hai aur kitna._

### 12. Keyboard Shortcuts & Find  `10:23`

_QuickBooks shortcut keys, menus aur search._

- `10:30` Press F1 any time for the full shortcut list: Ctrl+I invoice, Ctrl+J customers, Ctrl+A accounts, Alt+S save…  
  _F1 dabayein — saari shortcut keys ki list._
- `10:38` Date fields have QuickBooks keys too: T = today, + / - = next / previous day, M = month start, H = month end.  
  _Date field mein: T = aaj, + / - = agla/pichla din, M = mahine ka shuru._
- `10:51` Alt + the underlined letter opens a menu (Alt+F, Alt+C, Alt+U, Alt+R…). Use the arrow keys and Enter.  
  _Alt + underline letter se menu khulta hai. Arrow keys aur Enter._
- `11:00` Find (Ctrl+F) searches every transaction by number, name, memo, item or amount.  
  _Find (Ctrl+F): number, naam, memo ya amount se kuch bhi dhoondhein._

### 13. Multiple Companies  `11:10`

_Doosri company alag theme ke sath, aur dono mein switch._

- `11:16` Ctrl+O opens the Company List. Add a second company — ABC Toys — with the green theme.  
  _Ctrl+O: Company List. Doosri company ABC Toys hara (green) theme ke sath._
- `11:39` ABC Toys opens in green with its own empty books — separate customers, items and reports.  
  _ABC Toys green mein, apne alag books ke sath._
- `11:45` Use "Quick switch" in the title bar to jump between companies. Each one keeps its own colour.  
  _Title bar mein "Quick switch" se company badlein — har ek ka apna rang._
- `11:53` Back in XYZ Toys — red again.  
  _Wapas XYZ Toys — phir se laal._
- `11:59` You can change a company theme any time: Company › Theme & Appearance. Changes apply instantly.  
  _Theme kabhi bhi badlein: Company › Theme & Appearance._
- `12:09` Purple and Dark mode… and back to Ruby Red, light.  
  _Purple aur Dark mode… phir wapas laal._

### 14. Google Sheets & Backup  `12:17`

_Google Sheet database connect karein aur backup lein._

- `12:23` Right now data is saved on this computer ("Local only"). Paste your Apps Script Web App URL here to sync to Google Sheets.  
  _Abhi data isi computer par hai. Apps Script URL paste karein — Google Sheet se sync._
- `12:34` Setup Guide: create a Google Sheet, open Extensions › Apps Script, paste this code, deploy as a Web app for "Anyone".  
  _Google Sheet banayein, Extensions › Apps Script, ye code paste karein, Web app deploy._
- `12:45` Each company gets its own tabs in the sheet. Changes upload in the background and retry automatically when offline.  
  _Har company ke alag tabs. Internet na ho to baad mein khud upload._
- `12:51` File › Back Up Company downloads a full backup file; File › Restore Backup brings it back.  
  _File › Back Up se poora backup download; Restore se wapas._
